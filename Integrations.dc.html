How a connection is made.

  1. 01ConnectChoose a connection from the marketplace.
  2. 02Allow accessSign in to the platform and approve access.
  3. 03Select accountsPick the ad accounts or properties to include.
  4. 04ReviewCheck the selection before it is saved.
  5. 05SavedThe connection appears with its sync status.

After it is connected.

Manage

Change the selected accounts, run a sync and see its result, or disconnect. Imported history stays, marked as disconnected.

Recover

Expired access, a revoked permission, a missing account or a failed sync each show their own next step.

Control access

Turn a client's access off to pause a connection without removing it. Every change is written to an activity log.

An enquiry arrives

Someone taps an ad and sends a form. AnchorIQ stores the campaign on the lead, so the spend behind it can be traced later.

An agent makes contact

The lead lands in an agent's queue. First contact is timed against a 30 minute target, and a miss escalates to the team leader.

The lead is qualified

Location, property type, budget, timeframe and purpose are confirmed. Only now does the lead count as qualified.

A viewing is booked

The viewing goes in the diary and the next follow-up is set, so the lead always has a next action.

An offer is made

The offer is recorded against the lead. Managers watch this stage to see the weeks ahead.

The deal is won

The win is credited back to the campaign that started it. That is how AnchorIQ shows what a win costs.

Questions buyers ask first.

What does AnchorIQ connect to?

Planned connections cover CRM systems, property portals, advertising accounts, web analytics and spreadsheet imports. None is live yet. Tell us what you use and we will confirm what your rollout can include.

Is AnchorIQ only for real estate?

It is built for brokerages first. Branding, terminology, modules and workspaces are set per company, so a sales team outside property uses the same platform with the listing modules switched off.

Does AnchorIQ change records in our CRM?

No. AnchorIQ is reporting only by default. Writing stages or notes back to the CRM is planned as a setting you choose to turn on.

Who can see what?

Access follows role: agent, team leader, manager and management. Each role sees a different home screen and a different scope of leads, and you can rename the roles to match your organisation.

Are the screens on this page real data?

No. The dashboard screens and every chart on this page use sample data. Nothing here comes from a customer account.

How is pricing set?

By the size of your teams and the modules and connections you need. Once demo requests open, we will send a tailored proposal.
